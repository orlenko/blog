---
title: "Two Small Speech Utilities for the Walking Reading Pile"
date: 2026-05-DD HH:MM:SS -0500
---

I keep two reading piles. One I read sitting down, which is the small one. The other I read by listening, which is the large one — a backlog of articles, blog posts, and longer pieces I never quite get to at a desk.

The walking pile needed better infrastructure, so I wrote [aitts](https://github.com/orlenko/aitts): two small utilities for getting text into ears.

The first is a friendlier `say`. macOS's built-in text-to-speech is functional but plain, and this is `say` with a little more taste.

The second is an article-to-playlist tool. Hand it a list of URLs and it produces an audio queue you can stick in your headphones. I now do an embarrassing amount of my reading on walks. By the time the dog and I are back, I've cleared three articles and the dog has met every other dog on the block.
