# Product

## Register

Workshop. Bjola.org is a public, technical workbench: practical enough for
someone deciding whether to install a tool, personal enough to show why it was
built, and honest about unfinished edges.

## Users

- Developers looking for small, inspectable tools that solve concrete chores.
- Potential contributors looking for source, installation paths, maturity, and
  a clear account of what the software does.
- Technical readers following short notes about building and using the tools.

## Product Purpose

Give Bjola's public software one coherent home. The site should make useful
projects discoverable, distinguish shipped tools from experiments, and preserve
the deliberately low-friction blog workflow: one dated Markdown file, one push.

The site does not sell consulting services. Bjola.ca remains the corporate and
professional surface; Bjola.org is where the public work and the thinking around
it live.

## Brand Personality

Practical, curious, technically exact, modest, and slightly wry. It should feel
like visiting a capable builder's workbench, where labels are legible, tools are
used, and nothing has been polished into a showroom prop.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with a hero slogan, feature-card grid, and repeated
  calls to action.
- Corporate portfolio theatre or inflated claims about reach and maturity.
- Fake terminals, decorative dashboards, neon AI imagery, glassmorphism, and
  gradients used as a substitute for hierarchy.
- A content-heavy magazine that makes publishing a short note feel ceremonial.
- A visual clone of Bjola.ca. The two sites are siblings, not duplicates.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with working software.** A visitor should reach a repository, demo, or
   install path without decoding marketing copy.
2. **Label maturity honestly.** Status words describe observable availability,
   not enthusiasm, stars, or recent commit activity.
3. **Make the workshop visible.** Notes and experiments belong near shipped
   tools because the reasoning is part of the value.
4. **Keep publishing friction near zero.** A new note must remain a single
   Markdown-file change with automatic metadata, feed, sitemap, and share card.
5. **Own the essentials.** Required layouts, styles, and assets live in the
   repository; the site does not depend on a remote theme.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site is fully usable with a keyboard, exposes a strong visible focus state,
respects reduced-motion preferences, and maintains at least 4.5:1 contrast for
normal text. Status is always written in text and never conveyed by colour
alone. Layout and type remain readable at narrow mobile widths and at 200% zoom.
